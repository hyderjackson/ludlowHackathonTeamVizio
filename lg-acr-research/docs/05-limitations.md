# Evidence boundaries

- One rooted TV, firmware and selected upload path. No all-LG or stock-firmware generalization.
- Live Plus Off suppressed this path. It does not establish that every tracking service stopped, that consent was bypassed, or that the daemon always exited.
- Programme audio was present in the sampled source. The retained evidence does not confirm built-in microphone selection or exclude acoustic pickup/mixing. Missing logs cannot prove a microphone was never used.
- No controlled offline-disconnection or standby experiment. Near-current client timestamps cannot exclude queued data receiving new timestamps.
- No demonstrated video upload, complete production PCM pipeline, original waveform reconstruction or server recognition.
- Certificate behavior is scoped to the rooted uploader. Full trust history, wrong-host/expired certificates and clean stock replication were not independently established.
- The independent packet recorder reported zero kernel drops. That supports capture completeness but does not establish every displayed frame or exact physical toggle time.
- Haar, MinHash, LG/Alphonso integration, local source capture and prior ACR studies already exist. Worldwide priority has not been established.

The public data file contains selected aggregates. Raw evidence is private. A reviewer can inspect the code and structural example immediately; full empirical reproduction requires separately authorized access to the original captures and SDK.
